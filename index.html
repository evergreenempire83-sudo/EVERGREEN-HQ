About Evergreen Empire LTD

Evergreen Empire LTD is a technology driven music company focused on building scalable digital infrastructure for the global music industry.

We help artists, record labels, music companies, producers, and entertainment businesses distribute, protect, manage, and monetize their music catalogs across major digital platforms worldwide.

Our ecosystem combines music distribution, royalty intelligence, content protection, metadata management, and monetization systems into one connected infrastructure.

Our mission is simple: To help music businesses and artists build sustainable long term value through professional digital infrastructure.

What We Do

Integrated ecosystem built for artists, labels and music businesses worldwide.

Global Music Distribution

We deliver music to major streaming and download platforms worldwide including streaming services, social platforms, and digital music stores. Artists, labels, and music companies can manage releases, metadata, royalties, and catalog operations from one ecosystem.

Rights Management

We help artists, labels, and music companies organize and protect ownership rights connected to their music and intellectual property. This includes metadata management, catalog organization, ownership tracking, and rights infrastructure.

Royalty Processing

Our systems are designed to process royalty data with precision and transparency. We provide royalty ingestion tools, revenue tracking, analytics, and reporting systems for artists, labels, and catalog owners.

Content Protection

We support digital content protection systems designed to help rights holders identify and manage unauthorized usage of their content online.

Catalog Monetization

We help artists, labels, and music companies explore monetization opportunities across streaming, licensing, social media, sync opportunities, commercial usage, and digital campaigns.

Music Infrastructure

Evergreen Empire LTD is focused on building long term infrastructure for artists, labels, and music businesses. We believe the modern music industry requires scalable systems built for ownership, transparency, and growth.
